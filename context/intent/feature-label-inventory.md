# Feature: Label Inventory

## What

Inventories labels and highlights names, descriptions, icons, colors, and duplicates that do not meet the selected policy.

## Why

Well-organized labels make it easier for administrators to classify and locate resources consistently across an installation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Shows label names and available identifying details.
- [x] Identifies duplicates and selected policy violations.
- [x] Supports recording review progress.
- [x] Provides a direct route to manage labels in Home Assistant.
